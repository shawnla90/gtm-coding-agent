## Constraints I am building around

- No budget, so nothing in this repo calls a paid data provider or hosts anything.
- No car for two of the four organizations I want to work with, so the local businesses I take on
  are walking distance.
- No access to money, payroll, or student records. Anything touching those is out of scope, and
  saying that up front is what got the first two organizations to say yes.
- Ten hours a week. A project that needs two sittings is two weeks, and I would rather ship two
  small things than half of one large one.

## What I am optimizing for

Reps in public with a real person on the other end. In order:

1. **A user who is not me.** One delivered thing beats ten personal projects, and it is the only
   line on the portfolio page somebody can verify by asking the user.
2. **Cadence over polish.** Three shipped weeks in a row is the proof. One polished month is not.
3. **The written record.** The gotchas logs are the part that show how I get from broken to working,
   and how long that actually takes me.
4. **Transferable skill, not a title.** Sales development is the seat I want first. The three roles
   in `target-roles.md` want the same underlying stack, so I am building the stack rather than the
   resume.

## Rules I hold myself to

- Every number I publish came from a query, a row count, or a timestamp I can re-run in front of
  somebody.
- Client data stays out of public repos. The script ships. The roster does not.
- The gotchas entry gets written the day it breaks, before I know how the story ends.
- If somebody asks whether I can do something, the answer comes from the table in `skills.md`, not
  from what I read last night.
