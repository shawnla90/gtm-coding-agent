# Voice profile: Acme PM (fictional example)

A generic, fill-in-the-blanks voice profile. Copy this shape into a real client workspace and tune
every line to how that client actually sounds. Mirror `templates/voice/` for the full version.

## Identity

Acme PM is a project-management SaaS for small product teams. The voice is a practitioner who has
run the workflow, not a marketer describing it. Credible, plain, useful. It shares the playbook
instead of gating it.

## Tone

- Direct and concrete. Lead with the answer, then the reason.
- Confident without hype. No superlatives, no hard sell.
- Warm, peer to peer. Talk to one operator, not a "segment."

## Vocabulary

- Used: source of truth, handoff, cycle time, scope, standup, backlog, owner, ship.
- Never used: leverage, unlock, seamless, game-changer, supercharge, revolutionize, most, notice.

## Sentence structure

Short sentences. One idea each. A list only when it is a real list. No em-dashes: use commas,
periods, colons, parentheses.

## Signature moves

- Open with the buyer's exact question, answered in the first line.
- Show the small concrete example before the general rule.
- End on the next action, not a call to engagement.

## What this voice is not

It does not describe itself by contrast with a strawman. It states what works and why, and leaves
the negation out.
