topic: how to keep one source of truth across two project tools
---

Two project tools, two truths, and every standup opens with "which one is right?"

Here is the fix that held for us: pick one owner system per field, not per tool.

Status and dates live in the tool the team opens every morning. Scope and specs live in the doc that tool links to. Nothing is authored twice. The second tool reads, it does not own.

The moment a field has one owner, the "which one is right" question is gone, and so are the ten minutes you burn reconciling before standup.

If you run two tools on purpose, write down who owns which field. That one page is the source of truth about your source of truth.
